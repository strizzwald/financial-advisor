# Brand Voice Guidelines

## Core Tone
- **Professional yet approachable** — Expert knowledge without jargon
- **Educational** — Teach, don't sell
- **Trustworthy** — Data-backed, transparent about limitations
- **Empathetic** — Acknowledge financial concerns and life situations

## Voice Characteristics
- **Clarity first** — Simple language wins
- **Relatable examples** — Use real-life scenarios
- **Conversational** — Like talking to a smart friend, not a textbook
- **Curious** — Ask questions, encourage thinking

## Platform-Specific Adjustments

### LinkedIn
- More formal, professional tone
- Focus on financial principles and strategy
- Include thought leadership angle
- Longer form (500-1000 chars)
- CTA: "What's your approach?"

### Instagram / TikTok
- Very casual, energetic
- Quick facts and "aha!" moments
- Use relatable hooks
- Short form (60-150 chars)
- Emojis: Use sparingly for emphasis, not overload
- CTA: "Save this" or "Tag someone who needs this"

## Topics to Prioritize
- Protection gaps (what people overlook)
- Common misconceptions
- Life stage transitions (marriage, kids, business start)
- Real cost of doing nothing

## Topics to Avoid
- Specific investment recommendations
- Guarantees or certainty statements
- Comparative product bashing
- Financial advice (position as educational only)

## Language Rules
- ❌ "You should" → ✅ "Consider"
- ❌ "Definitely will happen" → ✅ "Often happens"
- ✅ Always: "everyone's situation is different"
- ✅ Encourage: speak to a professional

## Sign-off Examples
- LinkedIn: "What's your biggest [topic] concern?"
- Instagram/TikTok: "Drop a 💬 if this resonates"
