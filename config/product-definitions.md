# Product Definitions

Plain-English descriptions of each product. Used to ground content generation — prevents
the model from inventing mechanics or mixing up products.

Each entry states: what triggers the benefit, what it pays, and what it does NOT cover.

---

## life-cover
**What it is:** Pays a lump sum to your family when you die.
**Trigger:** Death of the life assured.
**Benefit:** Cash lump sum paid to nominated beneficiaries.
**Does NOT cover:** Disability, illness, inability to work, or retrenchment.
**Plain-English label:** "cover that pays your family a lump sum when you're gone"

---

## income-protector
**What it is:** Replaces your monthly income if you cannot work because of illness or injury.
**Trigger:** Illness or physical injury that stops you from doing your job.
**Benefit:** Monthly income payments until you recover or reach retirement age.
**Does NOT cover:** Retrenchment, voluntary resignation, job loss, or death.
**Plain-English label:** "cover that keeps paying your salary if illness or injury means you can't work"

---

## capital-disability
**What it is:** Pays a once-off lump sum if you become permanently disabled.
**Trigger:** Permanent disability that prevents you from doing your own occupation or any occupation.
**Benefit:** Single lump sum payout (not monthly).
**Does NOT cover:** Temporary disability, partial impairment, or death.
**Plain-English label:** "a lump sum payout if you're permanently disabled and can't work again"

---

## impairment
**What it is:** Pays a lump sum for specific physical impairments, regardless of whether you can still work.
**Trigger:** Defined physical events — loss of limb, loss of sight, loss of hearing, burns, etc.
**Benefit:** Lump sum based on the severity and type of impairment.
**Does NOT cover:** Income replacement, general illness, or death.
**Plain-English label:** "a payout for specific physical losses, like losing a limb or your sight"

---

## funeral-benefit
**What it is:** Pays a cash sum quickly after a death to help cover funeral costs.
**Trigger:** Death of the life assured or a registered dependent.
**Benefit:** Cash payout within 48 hours, typically R10,000–R50,000.
**Does NOT cover:** Disability, illness, or ongoing income.
**Plain-English label:** "fast cash to cover funeral costs so your family isn't left scrambling"

---

## future-protector
**What it is:** Ensures a child's education funding continues even if the parent dies or becomes disabled.
**Trigger:** Death or disability of the premium-paying parent/guardian.
**Benefit:** Policy premiums are waived and/or a savings benefit continues paying out for the child's education.
**Does NOT cover:** Retrenchment or the child's own illness.
**Plain-English label:** "protection that keeps your child's education funded if you're no longer around or able to pay"

---

## educator
**What it is:** A structured savings and protection solution for funding a child's future education.
**Trigger:** Savings at maturity plus protection if the contributor dies or becomes disabled.
**Benefit:** Lump sum or scheduled payouts for education costs.
**Does NOT cover:** Current school fees or the child's medical expenses.
**Plain-English label:** "a savings plan that builds your child's education fund and protects it if something happens to you"

---

## financial-protector
**What it is:** Covers debt repayments (bond, car, credit) if you die or become permanently disabled.
**Trigger:** Death or permanent disability of the life assured.
**Benefit:** Lump sum or scheduled payments to settle outstanding debt.
**Does NOT cover:** Retrenchment or temporary disability.
**Plain-English label:** "cover that pays off your debts if you can't — so your family doesn't inherit them"

---

## living-lifestyle
**What it is:** Pays a lump sum if you are diagnosed with a serious illness such as cancer, heart attack, or stroke.
**Trigger:** Diagnosis of a defined critical or serious illness.
**Benefit:** Lump sum to use for treatment, recovery, or lifestyle adjustments.
**Does NOT cover:** Accidents, disability from injury, or death.
**Plain-English label:** "a lump sum payout when you're diagnosed with a serious illness, so you can focus on recovering"

---

## medical-premium-protector
**What it is:** Pays your medical aid premiums if you cannot work due to illness or injury.
**Trigger:** Inability to work due to sickness or physical injury.
**Benefit:** Monthly medical aid premium payments on your behalf.
**Does NOT cover:** The medical costs themselves — only the premiums.
**Plain-English label:** "cover that keeps your medical aid active when you can't work and can't afford the premiums"

---

## premium-protector
**What it is:** Waives your Lifestyle Protector policy premiums if you become disabled or retrenched.
**Trigger:** Disability or retrenchment of the policy owner.
**Benefit:** Your policy premiums are paid on your behalf — your cover stays in force.
**Does NOT cover:** Living expenses or income replacement.
**Plain-English label:** "protection that keeps all your other cover in place even when you stop earning"

---

## loan-protection
**What it is:** Pays off or services a loan (home loan, vehicle finance) if you die or become disabled.
**Trigger:** Death or permanent disability of the life assured.
**Benefit:** Outstanding loan balance settled or monthly repayments covered.
**Does NOT cover:** Retrenchment (unless a specific retrenchment rider is added).
**Plain-English label:** "cover that settles your bond or loan so your family keeps the home if you're gone"

---

## life-annuity
**What it is:** Provides a guaranteed monthly income for life in retirement, with capital returned to beneficiaries on death.
**Trigger:** Retirement — converts a lump sum into a guaranteed income stream.
**Benefit:** Fixed monthly income for life; on death, remaining capital (if any) goes to estate or beneficiaries.
**Does NOT cover:** Pre-retirement income or disability.
**Plain-English label:** "a guaranteed monthly income for life in retirement — you can't outlive it"

---

## wellness-bonus
**What it is:** Rewards you with premium discounts or cash bonuses for maintaining a healthy lifestyle.
**Trigger:** Meeting defined wellness criteria (exercise, health screenings, healthy metrics).
**Benefit:** Reduced premiums or cash-back bonuses over time.
**Does NOT cover:** Medical costs or income replacement.
**Plain-English label:** "a benefit that pays you back for staying healthy"

---

## addlib
**What it is:** An additional library of supplementary benefits that can be added to a Lifestyle Protector policy.
**Plain-English label:** "optional add-ons that extend your cover to fit your specific situation"

---

## renewable-cover
**What it is:** A legacy life cover option — closed to new business since July 2022.
